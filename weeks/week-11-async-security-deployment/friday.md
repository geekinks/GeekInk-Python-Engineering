## Detailed lesson content

Production readiness means known configuration, tests, logs, health checks, backups, least privilege, and a rollback idea. A deployment is not complete until it can be observed and recovered.

Plan input, processing, output, and failure behaviour before coding. Make a small working version first; add complexity only after observing correct output.
